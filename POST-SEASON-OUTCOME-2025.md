## Final 2025 Season Outcomes — Injury Recovery Wins! ✅

### Ronald Acuña Jr. — Atlanta Braves
✅ **NL Comeback Player of the Year — November 2025**

- Returned from second left ACL tear in **May 23, 2025** — roughly 8 months post-surgery
- Finished 2025 regular season: **.290 BA | 21 HR | 95 games played**
- Named NL Comeback Player of the Year at MLB All-MLB Awards (Nov 2025)
- Braves reached the postseason

> Quote from Braves Country: "earn[s] NL Comeback Player of the Year after a strong return from injury" — batted .290 with 21 home runs in just 95 games despite the year-long absence.

### Mookie Betts — Los Angeles Dodgers
✅ **Recovered Fully — Named NL Player of the Week (Sept 15, 2025)**

- Lost ~20 lbs from undiagnosed viral illness in spring 2025; fully recovered
- Overcame "tough health journey" during early season — credited wife Brianna for support
- Named **National League Player of the Week on September 15, 2025**
- Dodgers defending World Series champions heading into postseason 2025
- Continuing to "make baseball look easy" heading toward another World Series appearance

---

### Freddie Freeman — Los Angeles Dodgers
✅ **All-Star, Walk-Off Grand Slam Hero — World Series Championship Repeat**

- Named an **All-Star for the 9th time** in his career during the 2025 season
- Hit **INCREDIBLE walk-off grand slam** in Game 1 of the 2025 WorldSeries (again!) — making him the **first player in MLB history with 2 career World Series walk-off home runs** (previously did it in 2024)
- Dodgers won back-to-back World Series titles
- 2025 season stats at injury time: .292 BA, .836 OPS, 10 HR, 49 RBI (all before wrist HBP recovered in July)
- Considered one of the most durable and important players in all of baseball — always returns from every minor injury incident quickly
