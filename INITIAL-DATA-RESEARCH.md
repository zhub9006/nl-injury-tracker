### Specific Injury Context:
- **Oblique (April 2026):** Suffered during a check swing vs Washington Nationals. Felt it while running bases. Roberts initially said "a couple weeks" was a fair timeline. Betts was later cleared for live BP reps and was pain-free but awaiting a rehab assignment before full activation.
- **Hand fracture:** Broke his left hand on a hit-by-pitch from Kansas City Royals' Dan Altavilla in the 7th inning — expected 6-8 week timeline. Dodgers compared it to Corey Seager's 2021 broken hand that cost 65 games. Betts was replaced on the roster by Miguel Rojas at shortstop, with Hyeseong Kim also called up. This hand injury was one of two significant injuries Betts suffered during the 2025 season, the other being the viral illness that sapped his strength late in the year.
- **Viral illness (March 2025, persisting into July):** Undiagnosed condition caused Betts to lose nearly 20 pounds in two weeks, induced vomiting, and prevented him from eating normally. Forced him to fly home from Tokyo ahead of the season-opening series against the Chicago Cubs. Blood tests and medical work came back normal. Betts returned for the Dodgers' 2025 home opener April 15-17, 2025 at Dodger Stadium vs the Detroit Tigers.
