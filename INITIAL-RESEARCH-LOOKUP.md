## 1. Ronald Acuña Jr. — Atlanta Braves (OF / DH, #13)

### Current Status: 🟡 On Rehab Assignment — Triple-A Gwinnett

**Primary Injury:**  
- **Left hamstring strain** — Grade 1, second occurrence (first sustained June 9, 2025 vs. Colorado Rockies). Pulled up lame sprinting to first base. Placed on 10-day IL. Moved to FCL Braves rehab, then transferred to Triple-A Gwinnett Chiefs on July 17–18, 2026.

**Resolved Secondary Injury:**  
- **Left fibula non-displaced fracture** — sustained March 3, 2026, from a collision at home plate vs. Phillies. Ruled to have healed completely; cleared for sprinting and cutting drills.

**Rehab Highlights:**
- All rehab gates (BP/throwing, straight-line running, cutting drills, fielding) CLEARED.
- Hit a majestic grand slam in his third Florida Complex League rehab game — a 423-foot blast clocked at 112.8 mph exit velocity.
- Made his Triple-A Gwinnett RF debut on July 19, 2026.
- Gwinnett stats through July 21: 4-for-16 (.250) with 1 HR in 3 games.
- Combined FCL + Gwinnett: 5-for-23 (.217) with 1 HR, 5 RBI, .904 OPS.
- Braves GM Alex Anthopoulos: *"We think getting him back is going to be big."*
- Acuña himself (Feb 2025 interview with WSBTV's Alison Mastrangelo): *"That's maybe 90–95% [running and cutting]. No, no, I don't think so! There are priorities, and I am feeling great!"*

**Expected MLB Return:** July 22–23, 2026 (pending final activation by Braves)

**Primary Injury:**  
- **Stiff lower back** — began July 11, 2025; treated as day-to-day. Not expected to go on the IL. Managed through back tightness and workload.

**Resolving Secondary Injury:**  
- **Right wrist soreness** — appeared early July 2026; resolved as of July 2, 2026 after treatment and rest. Missed just 1 game.

**Post-Viral Fatigue (Late July 2025):**
- Suffered an undiagnosed (suspected norovirus) illness that caused ~20–25 pounds of weight loss in roughly two weeks.
- Weight dropped from approximately 175 lbs to 150 lbs; has since recovered back to ~175 lbs.
- Blood work and medical tests returned normal results.
- Betts: *"I feel great. Like, my body feels great. I've been able to work out. I've been able to do pretty much everything but eat, which is strange. So the symptoms have kind of gone away, I just have to figure out how to get my stomach to kind of calm down."* (March 24, 2025)

## 3. Freddie Freeman — Los Angeles Dodgers (1B, #5)

### Current Status: 🟢 Active & Healthy — Playing Regularly

**Current Status:** No acute injury. All issues from the 2025 season have fully resolved. Chronic left ankle managed through load management (rest days, occasional limited at-bats).

**Resolved Injuries:**
- **Neck stinger** (August 27, 2025) — suffered during game vs. Cincinnati Reds. Stinger ran from neck into right shoulder. Scratched from that lineup but returned Friday.
  - Manager Dave Roberts: *"From what I understand, he feels a lot better. There's still some kind of lingering effects, but nothing compromising. It is something that we'll keep an eye on day-to-day, but I feel confident that he can get through it and won't have any setbacks."*
- **Left rib discomfort / scar tissue** (March 2025) — originated in Tokyo Dome during Opening Series vs. Cubs. Missed both Tokyo games. Resolved quickly; returned to lineup.
- **HBP wrist contusion** (July 2026) — hit by 88 mph sinker from Bryan Mburu vs. Brewers. No fracture. Played the next game.

**Chronic Issue — Ankle:**
- **Right ankle sprain** (Sep 2024) → arthroscopic debridement surgery (Dec 2024). Played through 2024 World Series.
- **Left ankle sprain** (Jun 2025) → managed with rest days; occasionally limited to 2 at-bats.
- Ankle issues are chronic and long-term; managed through load management rather than IR placement.

**Offseason Note (2025–2026):**
- Canada team director Nathan Hamilton reported a "health issue" post-season; GM Farhan Zaidi and Andrew Friedman confirmed no procedure expected. Freeman optimized his swing front-leg loading for 2026 — no surgery needed.
- 2026: Selected as NL All-Star Game starter.

**WS MVP Stats (2024):** .280/.355/.565, 9 HR, 36 RBI, 25 SB, walk-off grand slam in Game 1.

**Sources:** USA Today (Aug 27, 2025), Dodger Blue (Jordan McPherson), MLB.com, MLB Trade Rumors, LA Times, CBS Sports, Sportsnet, The Athletic (Fabian Ardaya), Forbes, Dave Roberts press conferences
