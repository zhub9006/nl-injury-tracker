## Player 3: Freddie Freeman — Los Angeles Dodgers (1B)

### Status: ⚠️ Day-to-Day (Active Roster; Immediate Return Expected)

#### Current Injuries & Incidents
1. **Neck Stinger / Nerve Issue (August 27, 2025 — Recurring):** "Stinger" injury shooting from neck into right shoulder; recurring in recent seasons; out Wed Aug 27; returned Friday Aug 29. No game beyond that one missed.
2. **Left Wrist Contusion — Hit by Pitch (July 20, 2025):** Hit by 88 mph sinker from José Quintana in bottom of 6th vs. Brewers; immediately pulled himself out of game (unusual for Freeman who "doesn't come out of games"); X-rays returned **negative** — contusion only. Returned to lineup next game.
3. **Left Ankle Strain (Ongoing Management):** Chronic issue managed through load management and positional shifts (occasional LF assignments)

#### Injury History Timeline (Recent)
| Date | Injury | Status |
|------|--------|--------|
| Aug 27, 2025 | Neck stinger / nerve issue radiating to shoulder | Out Wed, back Fri; day-to-day; recurring |
| Jul 20, 2025 | Left wrist contusion from HBP (88 mph sinker by José Quintana) | X-rays negative; returned next game |
| Jun 2025 | Ankle soreness / fatigue | Load managed with rest days; positional shifts |
| Oct 2024 | Fractured hamate bone (left wrist/hand) | World Series batting practice incident |
| Aug 2024 | Left thumb contusion | Minor; day-to-day |
| Sep 2024 | Right ankle debridement surgery | Resolved; Dec 2024 follow-up |

#### Medical Definitions (for Community Reference)
- **Neck Stinger (Brachial Plexus Neuropraxia):** Nerve issue emerging from the neck, often affecting shoulder and arm; typically caused by compression or stretching of the brachial plexus. Recurring nature warrants monitoring for cumulative effects.
- **Wrist Contusion (Bone Bruise):** Negative X-ray — no fracture. Typically recovery in days, not weeks.

#### Latest Official Comments
- **Dave Roberts (Aug 29, 2025) re: stinger:** "From what I understand, he feels a lot better. There's still some kind of lingering effects, but nothing compromising. I feel confident he can get through it."
- **Dave Roberts (Jul 21, 2025) re: wrist HBP:** "That one, I held my breath. We dodged a bullet there."

#### Team Impact
- 2025 All-Star, .292 AVG, .836 OPS, 10 HR, 49 RBI at time of wrist injury
- Manager: "To lose him longer, it would have been really tough to overcome."
