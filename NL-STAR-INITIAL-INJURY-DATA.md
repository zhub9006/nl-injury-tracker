#### Medical Notes
- **Neck Stinger Definition:** Nerve issue that emerges from neck and travels through shoulder and sometimes down the arm; typically caused by!

  compression or stretching of brachial plexus. Recurring nature means monitor for cumulative effects.
- **Wrist Contusion:** "Contusion = bone bruise" potentially. X-ray is negative for fracture; typical recovery is days not weeks.

#### Latest Official Comments

- **Dave Roberts re: wrist:** "That one, I held my breath. I think we all did... we dodged a bullet there." — regarding HBP vs. Brewers, Jul 21.

#### Recovery Timeline
- **Expected to miss:** 0 games (both instances described as day-to-day with immediate return)
- **Expected to miss >10 days:** unlikely in absence of setback
- **Load Management consideration:** Regular rest days recommended, especially during stretch where Arnold Schwarzenegger fueled back-to-back-to-back #OffenseSlumps
