## 1️⃣ Ronald Acuña Jr. — Atlanta Braves

### Key Article 1: MLB.com ("Braves cautious with Ronald Acuña Jr.'s rehab")
- **Source URL:** https://www.mlb.com/news/ronald-acuna-jr-knee-injury-update-2025
- **Date:** Early 2026
- **Key Findings:**
  - Braves have NOT revealed when Acuña will return from his latest knee surgery
  - Acuña is hitting ridiculously long batting practice homeruns while rehabbing
  - Manager Brian Snitker: *"He's probably as strong as he's ever been"*
  - Two surgically repaired knees (both ACLs)
  - May never steal as frequently as 2023 (73 SB franchise record)
  - Could still add more 40-homer seasons
  - Has been throwing, running drills in straight paths, facing live pitching in BP
  - Has NOT been cleared for cutting drills yet
  - Braves want to avoid the lingering discomfort that followed him throughout 2022

### Key Article 2: MLB.com ("Ronald Acuña Jr. activated from injured list")
- **Source URL:** https://www.mlb.com/news/ronald-acuna-jr-activated-from-injured-list
- **Date:** May 2025
- **Key Findings:**
  - Acuña placed on 10-day IL with LEFT HAMSTRING STRAIN on May 2 vs Rockies
  - Mechanism: Running to first base on a grounder to second (playing SHOELESS barefoot)
  - Activated ahead of schedule vs Marlins (wanted to activate Tuesday, moved earlier)
  - Acuña: *"I had to follow the protocol... one can see it as minor but it can cost you the whole season"*
  - Was slashing .252/.362/.378 before injury
  - Acuña: *"I felt like a kid about to debut... super anxious"*
  - Team (Braves) was 32-16 at time of activation

### Key Article 3: MLB.com ("Ronald Acuña Jr. hits grand slam during Braves rehab assignment")
- **Source URL:** https://www.mlb.com/news/ronald-acuna-jr-hits-grand-slam-during-rehab-assignment
- **Date:** During rehab
- **Key Findings:**
  - Grand slam in FCL rehab game
  - 423 ft, 112.8 mph exit velocity on grand slam
  - Braves needed him back for stretch run to win division

## 2️⃣ Mookie Betts — Los Angeles Dodgers

### Key Article 1: MLB.com ("Mookie Betts battling undiagnosed illness")
- **Source URL:** https://www.mlb.com/dodgers/news/mookie-betts-battling-undiagnosed-illness
- **Date:** March 2025
- **Key Findings:**
  - Undiagnosed illness causing ~18 lbs weight loss in two weeks
  - Vomiting every time he ate solid foods — only tolerated liquids (smoothies)
  - First noticed symptoms ahead of Dodgers' Cactus League finale on March 11
  - Took team trip to Tokyo for season-openers vs Cubs despite illness
  - Blood work and vital tests ALL came back clean
  - Started new medication to quell vomiting
  - By March 15: managed full day of workouts (hitting, grounders, throwing), kept down solid foods
  - Manager Dave Roberts: *"I do think that Mookie's turned a corner... He feels great on his way home and expects to play tomorrow. So right now we're in a good spot."*
  - Weight: 157 lbs down from 175 at start of spring training
  - Betts: *"It's just hard to fathom not eating and going to play a game... maybe I play uphill a little for the beginning of the season"*
  - Was still released from starting Team USA Tokyo series

### Key Article 2: MLB.com ("Mookie Betts returns from illness and struggles")
- **Source URL:** https://www.mlb.com/news/mookie-betts-returns-from-illness-and-struggles
- **Date:** Mid-2025
- **Key Findings:**
  - "*Betts has been hurt on multiple levels in 2025*"
  - Illness caused ~20 lbs loss in two weeks
  - Death of his stepfather — personal/emotional toll
  - Back injury forced him out of a game (vs Nationals) — Dave Roberts update
  - Six-time Gold Glove winner going through "wringer"
  - Post-viral: body "eating itself"

## 3️⃣ Freddie Freeman — Los Angeles Dodgers

### Key Article 1: MLB.com ("Freddie Freeman exits after hit-by-pitch on left wrist")
- **Source URL:** https://www.mlb.com/news/freddie-freeman-exits-after-hit-by-pitch-on-left-wrist
- **Date:** July 2025
- **Key Findings:**
  - Hit by 88 mph sinker from Jose Quintana (Brewers) on left wrist
  - Freeman took pitch, looked down at hands, slowly walked toward dugout
  - Manager Dave Roberts: *"He just doesn't come out of games... I didn't know why he was walking toward me. Then hearing him saying that it was his wrist and he can't continue. So yeah, really worried."*
  - X-rays came back NEGATIVE
  - Considered day-to-day with left wrist contusion
  - Roberts: *"Dodged a bullet"* (to losing him 4-6 weeks)
  - Freeman subsequently sat but then game if team opted not to DL him
  - Miguel Rojas took over 1B; Dalton Rushing also available

### Key Article 2: MLB.com (Freddie Freeman left wrist HBP follow-up)
- **Source URL:** https://www.mlb.com/news/freddie-freeman-hits-back-x-rays
- **Date:** Mid-July 2025
- **Key Findings:**
  - X-rays on the HBP came back negative
  - Bone contusion considered day-to-day
  - Dodgers low-risk on outcome

### Key Article 3: Dodger Blue ("Dodgers Injury Updates: Freddie Freeman, Tommy Edman, Max Muncy, Alex Call")
- **Source URL:** https://dodgerblue.com/dodgers-injury-updates-freddie-freeman-tommy-edman-max-muncy-alex-call/2025/08/30/
- **Date:** August 30, 2025
- **Key Findings:**
  - Freeman was out of lineup Wednesday Aug 27 due to neck stinger
    - Nerve issue from neck into right shoulder
    - Manager Roberts: *"From what I understand, he feels a lot better... some lingering effects but nothing compromising"*
    - Not expected to miss a game
    - Dodgers had off day Monday for recovery
  - Freeman returned to lineup Friday
  - Neck stinger described as recurring but typically one-game affair
  - Trackers had gotten more concerned from Braves Beat side because of Acuña

### Key Article 4: Yahoo Sports via AP News (Freddie Freeman neck stinger update)
- **Source URL:** https://sports.yahoo.com/article/dodgers-freddie-freeman-sidelined-recurring-222505536.html
- **Date:** August 27, 2025
- **Key Findings:**
  - AP News confirms: *"All-Star Freddie Freeman was out of the Los Angeles Dodgers lineup on Wednesday because of a nerve issue in his neck"*
  - Stinger affects nerves from neck to shoulder/arm
  - Kiké Hernández started at 1B in Freeman's absence
  - Freeman was hitting .302 average leading NLpace
  - 2 for 4 on Tuesday before stinger day
  - Roberts: *"giving him a couple days, with the off day tomorrow"*
