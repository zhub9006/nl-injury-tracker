## 🏥 Why This Database?

NL stars like Acuña Jr., Betts, and Freeman are among the most-impactable players in all of baseball — and among the most frequently injured at the same time. Tracking recovery progression, return timelines, and historical injury patterns helps:
- Fantasy baseball players plan lineups
- Bettors adjust public numbers
- Front offices benchmark time-table projections
- Scholars and medical professionals spot longitudinal trends
- The Coaching Staff Universal rehabilitation advocacy

This is community-maintained. Even small corrections/updates matter — the big picture improved dramatically with multiple eyes on the same player.
