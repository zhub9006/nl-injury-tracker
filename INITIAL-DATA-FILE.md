## 🚧 Ronald Acuña Jr. — Atlanta Braves (OF / DH)

### Summary
> Acuña is in the final phases of rehab from his left hamstring strain, progressing through the MLB-required recovery protocol after having already torn both ACLs. Since two ACL injuries, his speed has been permanently altered — but power potential remains intact, having already produced 73 stolen bases/41 HR seasons and two 41-HR outputs from limited full seasons. The Braves are being cautious, preferring he be fully ready for cutting drills before returning.

### Current Injuries
1. **Left hamstring strain (Grade 1, 2nd occurrence)** — Suffered May 2 vs Rockies running to first base. Placed on 10-day IL May 10. Cleared for rehab by ~May 10.
2. **Left hamstring strain (1st occurrence)** — Earlier in season, recovered within 15 days.
3. **Left fibula non-displaced fracture** — Suffered March 3, 2026 in Spring Training. Rehab COMPLETE. Resolved.
4. ACL history: Right ACL (June 2021), Left ACL (May 2024 surgery), Left meniscus surgery (Sep 2024), Left MCL sprain (Apr 2025), Calf strain (Jul 2025).

## 🟡 Mookie Betts — Los Angeles Dodgers (SS / 3B / OF)

### Summary
> Betts endured the most turbulent stretch imaginable for a star player — an undiagnosed stomach virus that caused vomiting, 18-lb+ weight loss and GI distress through Spring and early season; his stepfather's death; a back injury; and an oblique strain that sent him to the IL. He returned to form and has shown heavy bat involvement. Doctors cleared him for bloodwork and a medication strategy, and he returned physically at 157 lbs (down from 175 lbs spring baseline) before resuming full participation.

### Current Issues
1. **Undiagnosed stomach illness** — Vomiting for ~2 weeks, limited to liquids only. Lost ~18 lbs. Bloodwork and vital tests came back clean. Now resolving — manager said "Mookie's turned a corner," can keep down solid foods and feels great.
2. **Back injury** — Stiff back forced exit from game in the series vs Nationals. Managing day-to-day.
3. **Post-viral fatigue** — Down to 157 lbs from 175. Now back at playing weight and hitting well.

## 🟢 Freddie Freeman — Los Angeles Dodgers (1B)

### Summary
> The most durable of the three NL stars tracked. No current acute injuries. Minor issues include wrist contusion from HBP and recurring neck stingers. A chronic left ankle (and surgically repaired right ankle with debridement in Dec 2024) requires load management, measured mainly through rest days and occasionally reduced at-bat counts. He was a 2024 World Series MVP and is a blueprint model for health maintenance.

### Current Issues
1. **Left wrist contusion** — Hit by 88 mph sinker from Jose Quintana (Brewers). X-rays negative. Grade 1 contusion, day-to-day.
2. **Neck stinger** — Recurring episode vs Reds (Aug 2025). Resolved within days. Manager Roberts: nothing compromising, day-to-day.
3. **Chronic right ankle** — Underwent debridement surgery Dec 2024; monitored with load management.
4. **Left ankle** — Sprained June 2025, intermittently managed.
