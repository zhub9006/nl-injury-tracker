## Mookie Betts — Los Angeles Dodgers (SS / 3B / OF)

- **Current Injury:** Stiff lower back (Jul 11, day-to-day); Right wrist soreness (resolved Jul 2); Post-viral fatigue
- **Viral Illness:** Undiagnosed stomach illness in late July 2025 caused ~20-pound weight loss in two weeks, with nausea and vomiting. Illness worsened during the Tokyo spring trip, leading to being sent back to LA for recovery. Blood work and medical tests returned normal.
- **Rehab Status:** Day-to-Day (Active). NOT expected on IL.
- **Expected Return:** Within days (no IL expected)
- **Recent Performance:** On a 7-game hitting streak (.355 avg over last 15 games). Hit .304/.400/.554 in first 15 games but struggled to .637 OPS over the next 91 games due to physical and mental toll.
- **Mental Health:** Betts has spoken openly about the mental impact of the illness, describing feelings of "not having a chance" at the plate. He's now accepting a supporting-cast role and seems to be rediscopping his form.
- **Fantasy Impact:** Returning to playing weight and playing regularly. Should be a solid lineup fixture.
